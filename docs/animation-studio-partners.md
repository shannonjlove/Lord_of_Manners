## Recommended approach
The 30 finished plates and the 62-page PDF mean no studio has to design the film from scratch, so a **hybrid pipeline** (animated plates plus some hand-drawn work, with a smaller team) is realistic. Precedent: Higgsfield premiered a 15-minute hybrid human-AI animated film at SIGGRAPH 2026 ([AWN](https://awn.com/news/higgsfield-debuts-k-k-b-r-showcasing-hybrid-human-ai-animation-pipeline)).

- Pitch **Lion Forge** and **Martian Blueberry** as co-producers.
- Get hybrid-pipeline quotes from **Wild Wheat** or **ANIMWOOD** as a fallback.
- Cut a **60–90 second proof of concept** (narration + slow pans across the plates) before outreach. Studios respond to reels faster than to PDFs.
