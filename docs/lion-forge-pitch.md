# Pitching Lion Forge Entertainment

_Compiled 2026-11-14_

## Company snapshot
- Founded 2019; focuses mainly on **kids & family and YA**. Los Angeles HQ, Atlanta pre-production studio. ([About](https://www.lionforgeentertainment.com/about/))
- **Leadership:** David Steward II (Founder & CEO); Stephanie Sperber (President & Chief Content Officer); Kirsten Newlands (EVP, Production & Partnerships); Koyalee Chanda (SVP, Animation). Steward and Sperber personally produce projects.
- **Funding:** in September 2025, HarbourView Equity Partners led a $30M investment to help Lion Forge fund and own its IP. ([Advanced Television](https://www.advanced-television.com/tag/lion-forge/))
- **Slate:**
  - *Iyanu* (Cartoon Network / HBO Max; second feature premiered July 2026)
  - George R. R. Martin's *A Dozen Tough Jobs* (adult animated feature)
  - *Lostlings* (short-form YouTube project growing into a long-form series)
  - *ShootAround* (with WEBTOON)
  - A Penguin Young Readers partnership to adapt children's books
- **Representation:** Activist Artists Management; Jon Kanak (Lumify).

## Two things that shape the pitch
1. **No unsolicited submissions.** Their policy: no unsolicited submissions, no calls, no drop-offs ([Backstage](https://www.backstage.com/resources/detail/productioncompany/lion-forge-animation-87683/)). The pitch has to come through:
   - an entertainment attorney, agent or manager;
   - a warm introduction from the TV One / BET network;
   - in-person meetings at Kidscreen, MIPCOM/MIPJR, the Pan African Film Festival (*Iyanu* sequel was a 2026 Official Selection) or WonderCon.
2. **They buy IP that can grow beyond one format.** Sperber has said as much publicly ([ShootAround deal](https://www.advanced-television.com/2026/04/28/lion-forge-webtoon-partner-on-shootaround-movie/)). Frame *Lord of the Manners* as a property: the short is the pilot, the book already exists, and each of the four sisters (Sense, Etiquette, Decency, Courtesy) can carry an episode.

## Pitch package checklist
- [ ] **Logline:** one sentence, family-friendly
- [ ] **One-page synopsis:** full story, ending included
- [ ] **Pitch deck (12–15 slides):** world and tone; characters (Lord Chivalry, Goode, Common, the four sisters); why now; audience; comps (*Hair Love*); series/franchise roadmap
- [ ] **Proof-of-concept reel (60–90 s):** narration over the plates
- [ ] **The book:** the 62-page PDF ([../Lord_of_the_Manners.pdf](../Lord_of_the_Manners.pdf)) + a print copy
- [ ] **Creator bio:** creator/showrunner, *I Married a Baller* (TV One); story producer, *Girls Cruise* (VH1); supervising producer, *The Truth with Jeff Johnson* (BET)
- [ ] **Chain of title:** ownership of the poem and the art, plus the AI-plate disclosure and a plan to have human artists remake the plates
- [ ] **The ask + what you bring:** co-production / development financing / first-look; your producing role, any partial funding, an existing audience

## Adjusting for their audience
Some themes are heavy for a children's slate: the pandemic, a mother who abandons her children, "let it all hang out." Present a **family edition** for Lion Forge; keep the adult edge for festivals or a different partner.
