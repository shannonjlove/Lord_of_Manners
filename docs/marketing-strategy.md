# Marketing Strategy — Lord of the Manners

_Compiled 2026-11-14_

## Core idea
The **film** is the flagship; the **book** feeds it. A finished short gives festivals, press and clips that a self-published poem rarely gets on its own.

## Positioning
The poem is an allegory: Chivalry, Common, Sense, Etiquette, Decency and Courtesy are manners turned into characters, and the subtitle is *Near Death of a Legacy*. It plays to grandparents, parents and teachers more than to children.

**Pitch line:** *A fable about what we pass down, and what we lose when manners go "Common."*

## Audiences
| Audience | Angle | Format |
|---|---|---|
| Gift buyers | Grandparents, Mother's/Father's Day, graduations | Keepsake hardcover |
| Teachers & youth programs | Character education, etiquette schools, cotillions, mentoring groups, churches | Book + free discussion guide |
| Legacy / generational-wealth audiences | Ties to the Financial Influencers Network keynotes (Earl & Lynnette) | Live reading or film clip inside a legacy keynote |

## Channels
1. **30-day social series.** One stanza and one plate per day, with narration over a slow pan across the image. Post on Reels, TikTok, Shorts and Facebook. End each post on a cliffhanger (stanza 09, "He married Whoooooo?!", is ideal). By day 30 you have an audience and a trailer.
2. **Festivals.** Animated-short and Black film festivals (American Black Film Festival, Urbanworld, Pan African Film Festival, Houston-area festivals). Use "Official Selection" to drive book sales.
3. **Print-on-demand book.** IngramSpark (bookstores and libraries) and/or Amazon KDP. The PDF is 16:9 landscape, so pick a landscape hardcover trim size.

## Risk: AI-generated plates
If the plates were made with an AI image tool:
- The US Copyright Office generally does not protect purely AI-generated images. The poem text is protected; the plates may not be.
- KDP requires disclosure of AI-generated content.
- Some festivals restrict AI work or require disclosure.

**Mitigation:** re-draw, composite or animate the plates with human artists, and disclose the process openly.

## Suggested sequence
1. Start the 30-day series
2. Release the print-on-demand hardcover while the series builds an audience
3. Lynnette's audiences + teacher outreach with the discussion guide
4. Submit the finished short to festivals
