# Lord of the Manners: Near Death of a Legacy

The last rays of sunset pierced the chamber,<br>
Almost with a boom.<br>
Striking the brightly shining armor,<br>
That then illumined the room.

With none else to see it<br>
But the one tucked in the bed,<br>
Hope returned for an instant<br>
And out loud this is what he said:

“If this be my end,<br>
Only God you surely do know<br>
What becomes of my name<br>
My descendants please do show.”

Through the open window,<br>
Across the room the wind did sweep<br>
Blowing out the single candle,<br>
And him into a very deep sleep

The twins just born,<br>
Such a joy he couldn’t hide,<br>
Quickly turned to sadness<br>
When he learned his wife Goode had died

The eldest he named Lai Dee,<br>
She had her mother’s flair.<br>
Her brother was Gent El<br>
And looked after his sister with great care.

Happiest of families they were,<br>
Until the twins did disappear.<br>
Everyone knew they’d gone,<br>
But no one knew where.

A widespread pandemic<br>
Largely took the blame<br>
And concerned for his family’s future,<br>
The man met a new paramour — a new dame

Where she came from<br>
All wondered but no one really knew<br>
Only on their wedding day did people learn<br>
He married Whoooooo?!

No qualities like Goode<br>
Or Lai Dee did she share<br>
Didn’t tend her home,<br>
And never a slip did she wear.

After much time had passed,<br>
And it was a rather long while,<br>
The news rang out<br>
That she was soon to have his third child.

Not much like her siblings,<br>
But she still did quite stand out.<br>
She never minded any manners,<br>
But instead let it all hang out.

Her named was Common<br>
Even long before she became of age.<br>
But disapproval would fuel her passions<br>
And fill her deep with rage.

Even before adulthood<br>
She was quite the precocious kid.<br>
And much like her name suggests,<br>
Common does just as Common did.

One night she fled far<br>
From all she’d known before.<br>
Years later she’d return<br>
With some children totaling four.

Hardly a trace was evident<br>
Of their lineage of renown.<br>
In dark eyes only<br>
A hint of their mother could be found.

That very same night<br>
Did a familiar pattern begin<br>
When away went their mother,<br>
Off again into the wind.

Left alone with their grandfather,<br>
Such a Common sin.<br>
He was determined not to lose<br>
Not this time and not ever again,

So the very next morning<br>
A plan he made and at once did begin<br>
To guarantee a sure victory<br>
And that they would all win

Names they hadn’t any;<br>
At least none that made any sense.<br>
The eldest great with money<br>
So in charge she was of all dollars and even cents.

Her sister had a knack<br>
For having incomparable flair.<br>
Never bending her back,<br>
Silk and lace ribbons always adorned her hair.

Sister number three<br>
Was as polite as she can be.<br>
With pleasant interaction<br>
She greeted everyone so happily.

Last not least<br>
Was sister number four,<br>
Never rushing to be first,<br>
She always let others go ahead before.

Returning favors and known for reciprocity<br>
So grandfather decided on a name<br>
And he called her Courtesy<br>
And her character was just the same

Such fine women<br>
Did they all grow up to be<br>
So wonderful in fact,<br>
That their mother returned just to see.

Great love did her father shower,<br>
Without a hint of despise,<br>
Though her daughters didn’t know her<br>
And her face they didn’t recognize.

Without any memory,<br>
Their mother did they disavow.<br>
Even the one called Etiquette<br>
Refused to curtsy or even bow.

Sense refused to acknowledge her<br>
As their mom or even distant kin.<br>
Courtesy and Decency were left to wonder<br>
All the places she must have been.

Waking from his sleep<br>
Did his mind no longer falter.<br>
He was ecstatic to be surrounded<br>
By his lovely granddaughters

In their faces a bright future<br>
He now could clearly see<br>
And happily ever after<br>
Were the future days in the life of Lord Chivalry.
